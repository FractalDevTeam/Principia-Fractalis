\markboth{REVISION 2}{REVISION 2}

This edition revises the first complete manuscript of \emph{Principia Fractalis}
against an extensive Lean~4 and Rocq (Coq) formalization effort carried out
since. The companion corpus now elaborates as a single verified environment
(\texttt{lake build PF}, kernel axioms \texttt{[propext, Classical.choice,
Quot.sound]} only, zero project-level axioms), and the substrate result is
independently re-verified in a second proof assistant. Nothing in this book is
submitted or published as settled beyond what that formalization establishes.

\section*{The rigor ledger governs every claim}

Each substantive claim in this book carries one of four labels, and the reader
should hold the book to them:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{PROVEN (kernel-verified)} --- machine-checked in Lean~4 (and, where
noted, Rocq), depending only on the three standard kernel axioms, with no
project axiom, \texttt{sorry}, or \texttt{native\_decide}.
\item \textbf{CONDITIONAL REDUCTION} --- machine-checked as an \emph{implication}
``if [a named, \emph{open} proposition] then [the claim]''. The implication is
verified; its hypothesis is not.
\item \textbf{ASSERTED (not derived)} --- a definition or chosen value, matched to
evidence but not derived from first principles.
\item \textbf{EMPIRICAL HYPOTHESIS --- UNTESTED} --- a physical or clinical claim
that only experiment can settle; a proof assistant cannot establish it.
\end{itemize}

\section*{What is PROVEN}

The load-bearing verified result is the \textbf{substrate rigidity theorem}: the
Timeless Field $\mathcal{T}_\infty$ is the uniformly hyperfinite $C^\ast$-algebra
of supernatural number $3^\infty$ --- \emph{forced}, not chosen. This is Glimm's
1960 UHF classification specialised to $3^\infty$, formalised here, to our
knowledge, for the first time in any proof assistant, and now carried in both
Lean~4 and Rocq. The unique-trace condition is \emph{derived} as a consequence
rather than assumed. The supporting operator-algebra results ($C^\ast$-simplicity,
trace faithfulness, the block-diagonal amplification classification) are
kernel-clean on the same footing.

\section*{What is a CONDITIONAL REDUCTION --- and the honest statement on the Clay problems}

\textbf{This book does not claim to have solved any Clay Millennium Prize
Problem.} The Millennium chapters (Riemann Hypothesis, P versus NP,
Yang--Mills, Navier--Stokes, Hodge, Birch--Swinnerton-Dyer) are machine-checked
\emph{conditional reductions}: each is an implication whose hypothesis is a
named open proposition carried explicitly in the corpus
(e.g.\ \texttt{PolylogEigenvalueConjecture} for P versus NP,
\texttt{VortexStretchingBoundedHypothesis} for Navier--Stokes, the
Hilbert--P\'olya and spectral-surjectivity conjectures for the Riemann
Hypothesis). The reduction is verified; the antecedent is open. Each chapter's
own verification ledger states this in that chapter's terms --- for instance,
``The Riemann Hypothesis is \emph{not} proven here and remains open.'' Where the
corpus formalises a Millennium statement unconditionally, it is a bounded or
classically-known instance (for example, the Riemann Hypothesis restricted to a
finite rectangle, classically known since Riemann), and its value is that it is
\emph{formalised}, not that it advances the open problem.

\section*{What is ASSERTED or EMPIRICAL}

The nine-value $\alpha$-skeleton is \emph{asserted}: the values are chosen and
matched to numerics (and, where anchored, to settled external mathematics such
as Perelman's resolution of Poincar\'e), not derived from the substrate alone.
The one genuinely non-circular, kernel-verified selection mechanism is the
base-3 abscissa $\sigma(\alpha)=\log_3\lvert 1+2\cos(\pi\alpha)\rvert$. The
consciousness threshold $\mathrm{ch}_2 \ge 0.95$, the clinical accuracy figures,
and all consciousness quantifications are \textbf{empirical hypotheses --- untested}:
they await experiment and are not established by any computation in this book.

\section*{New results in this revision}

Revision 2 is not only a scope repair. The formalization effort produced
mathematics the first edition did not contain, and two spine arrows advanced
from nothing to machine-checked content. Each result below depends on exactly
the three kernel axioms, with no \texttt{sorry} and no \texttt{native\_decide}.

\paragraph{Emergent locality: five of the six structural axioms of a local net.}
Before this revision, ``emergent locality'' appeared nowhere in the manuscript
and nowhere in the formal corpus. It now carries a net of algebras over an
arbitrary set of sites,
$\mathcal{A}(S) = \mathrm{alg}\langle \bigcup_{i \in S} \mathrm{ran}\,\lambda_i \rangle$
inside $\bigotimes_{i \in \iota} M_3(\mathbb{C})$, satisfying \emph{isotony},
\emph{locality}, \emph{additivity} and the \emph{empty-region} condition
unconditionally, and \emph{completeness} for a finite site set. Because the
index set is arbitrary, this holds uniformly at every tower level
$M_{3^k} \cong \bigotimes_{i<k} M_3$ rather than level by level.

The substantive point is that locality is a \emph{consequence} of the substrate
being a tensor product over sites, not an axiom imposed on it: for $i \neq j$
the proof reduces to $a\cdot 1 = 1 \cdot a$ at one slot and $1 \cdot b =
b \cdot 1$ at the other. In algebraic quantum field theory the net is logically
prior to spacetime, which is recovered from it --- so this is the structure the
$\mathcal{M}^4 = \mathrm{Aut}/\mathrm{Aut}_0$ route needed and did not have.
\textbf{No spacetime is produced}: regions are subsets of an index set, with no
metric, no causal order and no dimension count. Covariance, the sixth axiom, is
absent and is blocked on the $\mathrm{Aut}(\mathcal{T}_\infty)$ classification
this book already records as open. Completeness for an \emph{infinite} site set
is open --- neither proved nor refuted --- and is recorded as open.
See Chapter~\ref{ch:timeless-field}, \S\ref{sec:emergent-locality}.

\paragraph{Affine algebraic de Rham: $d \circ d = 0$.}
For an arbitrary commutative $R$-algebra $S$, with no smoothness, finiteness or
regularity hypothesis: the forms $\Omega^n = \bigwedge^n_S \Omega[S/R]$, the
universal derivation $d_0$, a wedge product that is $S$-bilinear and
antisymmetric in degree one, a differential $d_1 : \Omega^1 \to \Omega^2$
obtained by descending across the Kähler presentation's spanned kernel, the
classical formula $d_1(a \cdot dy) = d_0a \wedge d_0y$, and
$d_1(d_0 a) = 0$. The differential is $R$-linear and $S$-Leibniz, which is
precisely why the obvious route through the exterior power's universal property
cannot define it --- that equivalence produces an $S$-linear map.

\textbf{There is no de Rham complex here and no de Rham cohomology.} The
general $d_n$ for $n \ge 2$ does not exist: a graded anti-derivation is absent
from the formal library under every spelling searched, and the only prepared
tool for mapping out of $\bigwedge^n$ rejects exactly the maps $d$ is. Nor can
the three-term complex be packaged, because $d_1$ is not $S$-linear and no
$R$-module structure is available on the exterior power at this pin. This work
is the \emph{local} building block of algebraic de Rham cohomology; gluing it to
a variety is unpriced, with the sheaf-cohomology layers it needs absent
entirely. \textbf{It does not touch the Hodge conjecture.}

\section*{Negative results, which are also results}

Several of this revision's findings are refutations, and they are entered as
findings rather than buried as caveats.

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{A ceiling over every $\alpha$ in the Yang--Mills chapter.} The
mass gap $\Delta = 420.43$~MeV is refuted three independent ways by this
book's own analysis: the resonance zero $\omega_c$ does not exist, because
$\mathcal{R}_f(2,s) = \zeta(s)$ has no positive real zero; the displayed
product is $132.148$, not $420.43$; and the corrected resonance condition caps
the chapter's own gap formula at $\Delta_{\max} = 62.3$~MeV \emph{over every}
$\alpha$. That ceiling is the finding: the failure is not a poor choice of
$\alpha$, and no choice rescues the mechanism.
\item \textbf{Three of four experimental predictions do not confirm the
framework.} Formalising Chapter~\ref{ch:geometric-unity}'s predictions showed
the cosmological lithium prediction \emph{fails} (the mechanism gives
$0.7016$ where $0.3053$ is required); the muon $g-2$ agreement is a
one-parameter fit with zero degrees of freedom, the scale having been
reverse-engineered from the observed value; and the ANITA energies differ by
exactly one rung of the base-3 ladder rather than agreeing. Only the Hubble
reconciliation survives, and it lands about $1.03\sigma$ above the local value.
\item \textbf{Navier--Stokes is not a settled anchor.} Clay lists the problem
\emph{Active}; the one Clay-aimed claim is contested, with its formal
artifact's correspondence to its manuscript disputed in print; and the rest of
the external chain concerns model equations, weak solutions or the forced
regime. The framework therefore has \textbf{one} settled external anchor
(Poincaré), not two, $\alpha_{\mathrm{NS}}$ is asserted rather than anchored,
and every value derived ``from the two anchors'' is conditional on one external
result plus one assertion of the framework. The ``settled anchor'' framing was
an error of ours, not of the cited authors.
\end{itemize}

\section*{The state of the formal corpus, stated as a number}

The companion corpus contains 8591 Lean files, of which 1477 lie in the
transitive import closure of the root module --- those are the ones this book
cites. Across all 8591, at code level, there are \textbf{zero} occurrences of
\texttt{sorry}, \texttt{sorryAx}, \texttt{native\_decide},
\texttt{ofReduceBool} and \texttt{axiom}, established two independent ways
after a first measurement was found to have been reported from a broken search.

Of the 1477, \textbf{933 pass the full gate} --- olean currency, the
three-axiom bar, no escape hatches, no dead binders, no dead or vacuous
premises, non-vacuity witnesses, and semantic hygiene --- and \textbf{537 do
not}, failing on the semantic criteria rather than on soundness. That gap is the
honest remaining distance of the formalization, it is not hidden, and the
figure above understates it in one direction: the non-vacuity check was found on
2026-10-27 to have been unable to see witnesses whose statements wrap across
lines, so more modules satisfy it than the count credits.

\section*{Principal corrections in this revision}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Supersessions.} The Lean development refined several numerical
values that the first edition carried as best-available. The spectral gap and the
NP ground-state energy are stated at their certified values, with the closed
forms cited symbolically so that future refinement has an anchor; downstream
figures that scale with them are recomputed.
\item \textbf{Errata.} A small number of first-edition statements were found false
as written (a base-3 modular identity, several stale $\alpha$-preview values, a
string-tension figure) and are corrected, with the corrected proofs machine-checked.
\item \textbf{Scope repairs.} Prose that outran its proofs --- most consequentially
the empirical ``validation'' narratives --- is scoped to what the source actually
establishes. The computational ``143-problem'' panel, in particular, is a
local-simulator run of a classical resonance formula, not quantum-hardware
evidence, and is presented as such.
\item \textbf{De-placeholdering.} Several formal statements that had stood as
typed placeholders now carry genuine content with explicit non-vacuity witnesses.
\end{itemize}
