\paragraph{Verification status (2026-08-02).} Two corrections to how
this appendix has previously been read. \textbf{(1) The Coq column is
structural-shape parity, not independent verification}: the legacy
Coq mirrors are of the form \texttt{Theorem name : True. Proof. exact
I. Qed.} (Appendix~L records this explicitly), so completing the Coq
build confirms the declarations exist, not that the mathematics is
re-proved; the genuinely re-proved Coq layer is
\texttt{PF\_Coq\_Code/PF\_Real/} (110 theorems, every
\texttt{Print Assumptions} closed). \textbf{(2) A clean Lean
elaboration of a theorem does not upgrade its scope}: each entry
retains the honest-scope status of its source chapter's ledger ---
CONDITIONAL REDUCTION entries remain conditional, and entries whose
carrier is substrate-restricted remain so. Ch.~34's master ledger
governs; this appendix is a map, not a certificate.
