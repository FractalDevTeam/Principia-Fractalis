\item \textbf{Dirichlet eta extension — complementary zero-structure
(rev 2, 2026-11-18).}  The Euler identity
$\eta(s) = (1 - 2^{1-s}) \zeta(s)$ lifts the Dirichlet eta function
to a total analytic object on all of~$\mathbb{C}$.  The 2026-11-18
chain delivers, on the three-axiom bar
$[\mathtt{propext}, \mathtt{Classical.choice}, \mathtt{Quot.sound}]$:
(a) the filled extension $\widetilde{\eta}_{\mathrm{ext}}$ is entire
(Differentiable~$+$~AnalyticOnNhd on~$\mathbb{C}$); (b) a full
pointwise iff characterisation of its zeros,
$\widetilde{\eta}_{\mathrm{ext}}(s) = 0 \iff 2^{1-s} = 1 \lor
\zeta(s) = 0$; (c) the ``extra'' zeros (where the factor vanishes)
form the countably infinite family
$s_k = 1 + 2\pi i k / \log 2$, $k \in \mathbb{Z}$, lying exactly on
the critical line $\mathrm{Re}\,s = 1$ and disjoint from the
$\zeta$-zeros; (d) no $\widetilde{\eta}_{\mathrm{ext}}$-zeros on
$\mathrm{Re}\,s > 1$ and no nontrivial zeros on $\mathrm{Re}\,s \ge 1$.
This is \emph{complementary} to the $\xi$-box march: it pins the
geography of $\eta$-zeros across the whole plane rather than
certifying positivity over a growing box.  The strand neither
discharges RH nor claims to --- the ``nontrivial'' $\eta$-zeros
live in $\mathrm{Re}\,s < 1$ and coincide there with the Riemann
$\zeta$-zeros off the extra family.

\subsection{Dirichlet eta extension: companion zero-structure (rev 2, 2026-11-18)}
\label{sec:ch20-rev2-eta-structure}

Parallel to the $\xi$-box march, the 2026-11-18 session landed the
full pointwise zero-structure of the Dirichlet eta extension
\[
\widetilde{\eta}_{\mathrm{ext}}(s) = (1 - 2^{1-s}) \zeta(s),
\qquad s \in \mathbb{C},
\]
with the removable singularity at $s = 1$ filled to Euler's classical
value $\widetilde{\eta}_{\mathrm{filled}}(1) = \log 2$.  Every theorem
below carries axioms exactly
$[\mathtt{propext}, \mathtt{Classical.choice}, \mathtt{Quot.sound}]$;
no \texttt{sorry}, no \texttt{native\_decide}, no project axioms.
The 16 Lean modules under \texttt{PF/DirichletEta*\_2026\_11\_18.lean}
and the generic Weierstrass Selmer helpers are the architecture.

\paragraph{Entire and analytic everywhere.}
Commits \texttt{7b2f9946}, \texttt{6131c331}, \texttt{35ef6f1f},
\texttt{4f603cc4}:
\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{dirichletEtaExtFilled\_differentiableAt\_atOne}
  --- removable-singularity closure at $s = 1$ via mathlib's
  \texttt{analyticAt\_of\_differentiable\_on\_punctured\_nhds\_of\_continuousAt},
  routing the derivative of the factor $(1 - 2^{1-s})$ at $s = 1$
  (equal to $\log 2$) through \texttt{HasDerivAt.const\_cpow} and
  pairing it with \texttt{riemannZeta\_residue\_one}.
\item \texttt{dirichletEtaExtFilled\_differentiable} ---
  $\mathrm{Differentiable}\,\mathbb{C}\,\widetilde{\eta}_{\mathrm{filled}}$,
  i.e.\ honestly entire.
\item \texttt{dirichletEtaExtFilled\_analyticOnNhd} and

\item \texttt{dirichletEtaExtFilled\_eventually\_ne\_zero\_near\_one}
  --- the filled value $\log 2$ is nonzero, so the extension is
  nonzero in a neighbourhood of~$s = 1$.
\end{itemize}

  the factor satisfies $|1 - 2^{1-s}| \ge 1 - 2^{1 - \mathrm{Re}\,s}
  > 0$ for $\mathrm{Re}\,s > 1$ (via

  $\mathtt{Real.rpow\_lt\_one\_of\_one\_lt\_of\_neg}$), and

\end{itemize}

\paragraph{What this strand is and is not.}
This chain pins the \emph{full pointwise zero structure} of
$\widetilde{\eta}_{\mathrm{ext}}$ across all of~$\mathbb{C}$, and it
localises the nontrivial zeros --- those not in the explicit extra
family on $\mathrm{Re}\,s = 1$ --- to the half-plane
$\mathrm{Re}\,s < 1$.  That half-plane contains the critical strip,
and the nontrivial $\eta$-zeros there are, by the zero-correspondence
theorem, \emph{exactly} the Riemann $\zeta$-zeros off the extra
family.  The chain therefore \emph{transposes} the RH question from
$\zeta$ to $\widetilde{\eta}_{\mathrm{ext}}$ without closing it:
every nontrivial $\widetilde{\eta}_{\mathrm{ext}}$-zero lies on the
critical line iff RH holds.

Combined with the $\xi$-box march (Section~\ref{sec:ch20-rev2-frontier})
and the operator-spectral reduction (Sections earlier), the
2026-11-18 strand gives a \emph{third} rigorous route into the
critical-strip zero-geography: a complete pointwise characterisation
at the level of $\widetilde{\eta}_{\mathrm{ext}}$ rather than of
$\xi$ or of $\tilde{T}_3^{\textsf{sym}}$.  None of the three strands
closes the Clay statement.
