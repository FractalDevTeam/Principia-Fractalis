This revision accompanies substantial work on the Lean~4 formalization of the book's
central claims. As of 2026-04-22 (GitHub master commit \texttt{c11a496}):

\section*{Lean 4 layer (\texttt{PF\_Lean4\_Code/PF/})}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{16 axioms remain} (down from 41 at the start of this revision cycle).
\item \textbf{Zero \texttt{sorry} placeholders} anywhere in the library.
\item \texttt{lake build} completes all 5486 jobs cleanly.
\item During rev~2, \textbf{four latent unsoundness bugs} were identified and corrected.
  These were axioms whose stated content was provably inconsistent with definitions
  already present in the same file:
  \begin{itemize}
  \item \texttt{empty\_tape\_bound} (claimed $\log(2^s \cdot 3^h) \leq 0$, false at $s=1$)
  \item \texttt{characteristic\_cylindrical\_round\_trip} (contradicted the
        \texttt{normalized} field of \texttt{CharacteristicFunctional})
  \item \texttt{cylindrical\_measure\_fourier\_is\_characteristic} (same
        contradiction pattern)
  \item \texttt{nuclearity\_essential} (contradicted a trivial \texttt{NuclearSpace}
        witness construction that also lives in the codebase)
  \end{itemize}
\item \textbf{Classical results now machine-checked as theorems} (no longer axioms):
  $\sqrt{2}$ and $\varphi$ interval bounds; radix-economy monotonicity
  $\big(Q_{\text{decreasing}\geq 4}$, $Q_4 \geq Q_b\big)$; positive-definite
  Hermitian property $\big(C(-s) = \overline{C(s)}\big)$; and
  $\|C(s)\| \leq 1$ for PD-normalized functionals. The \texttt{IsPositiveDefinite}
  definition itself was strengthened from a weak $.re \geq 0$ formulation to the
  standard real-and-non-negative formulation, unlocking these proofs.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Yang--Mills cluster: honest placeholder disclosure}

The Yang--Mills-related theorems in \texttt{PF\_Lean4\_Code/PF/YangMillsMeasure.lean}
(and one in \texttt{GaussianModel.lean}) are now theorems with explicit
\textbf{``\(\triangle\) CURRENT PROOF CAVEAT''} docstrings. They are proven against
the current placeholder \texttt{CovarianceOperator.quadraticForm~:=~0} and therefore
establish the degenerate (zero-covariance) Gaussian, not the genuine non-trivial
Yang--Mills Gaussian. The scientific content that remains unproven is clearly
delimited; see Chapter~23 \S``Status of Analytical Construction'' for details.
