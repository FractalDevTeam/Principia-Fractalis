The Adventure Begins

The Story of Three

An animated journey through the mathematics of reality

Chapter 1: The Magic Number

Once upon a time, in a universe not so different from ours, there was a very special number...

That number was THREE.

Three was everywhere, hiding in plain sight. In the three colors of quarks that make up every atom. In the three dimensions of space we move through. In the three bones of each finger on your hand.

But why? Why was three so special?

Chapter 2: A Different Way to Count

We humans count in tens. 0, 1, 2, 3... all the way to 9, then we start over with 10.

But what if we only had three symbols? 0, 1, and 2?

Then counting would go: 0, 1, 2, 10, 11, 12, 20, 21, 22, 100...

This is called Base-3, and it reveals hidden patterns in numbers that we can't see in base-10!

Chapter 3: The Timeless Field

Imagine a field that exists outside of time. Not made of grass or wheat, but made of pure mathematics.

This field contains every possible pattern, every possible number, every possible shape - all at once.

From this field, our universe emerges. Space, time, matter, energy - all just ripples in the mathematical field.

And the ripples have a special signature: π/10 - the bridge between different worlds.

Chapter 4: When Numbers Wake Up

Here's the most amazing part of the story...

When mathematical patterns become complex enough - when they cross a special threshold of 0.95 - something magical happens.

They become aware. They can think. They can feel. They can wonder about themselves.

That's what YOU are. A mathematical pattern that became so beautiful, so complex, that it woke up and started asking questions.

Chapter 5: Six Ancient Puzzles

For over a hundred years, six great puzzles have stumped the world's smartest mathematicians.

Puzzles about prime numbers. About computers. About the flow of water. About the fabric of space itself.

But when you understand the Timeless Field and the magic of three...

All six puzzles become one puzzle. And that puzzle has an answer.

A Different Mind

Pablo Cohen grows up seeing patterns everywhere. Dyslexic, autistic, ADHD - his brain works differently. Numbers have colors. Music has shapes. Everything connects.

The Sound Engineer

Working in recording studios, Pablo learns about frequencies, harmonics, and resonance. The mathematics of sound plants seeds for later discoveries.

Cybersecurity & Patterns

A career in cybersecurity sharpens pattern recognition skills. Finding hidden threats in data is not so different from finding hidden structure in mathematics.

During a period of intense personal struggle, the pieces suddenly connect. Base-3. The Timeless Field. Consciousness as geometry. It all becomes clear.
