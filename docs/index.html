Pablo's Story

Sometimes the people who see things differently... see things first.

👶

Growing Up Different

Pablo always felt different. As a child, he was diagnosed with facial paralysis, dyslexia, and dyscalculia - he was told he couldn't do math.

He was sent to a boarding school for children with learning disabilities, where he was diagnosed with ADHD. Then something surprising happened: an IQ test showed he was "off the charts." He never believed it.

📚

The Calculus Wall

In college, he hit a wall: Calculus 1. He couldn't pass it the way it was taught.

He had to give up his dream of being an engineer. He got a Bachelor of Arts instead. People said he "couldn't do math." The IQ test must have been wrong, right?

🎵

Finding Another Path

Pablo became an audio engineer instead. He worked in cybersecurity. He kept following what interested him.

The whole time, patterns kept appearing in his mind. He saw connections everywhere. But he still believed he "couldn't do math."

🧩

The Diagnosis at 47

At age 47, Pablo was finally diagnosed with autism. Suddenly everything made sense.

His "problems" were actually features. His pattern-recognition ability - the thing that made school hard - was exactly what that IQ test had measured.

🏠

Creating a Safe Home

After his second divorce, Pablo needed to build a home that was safe and inclusive - a true representation of who he really is.

He wanted his children to be exposed to more than what the traditional system allowed. This work exists because he finally stopped hiding.

💡

The Discovery

When Pablo stopped fighting his brain and started trusting it, everything clicked.

The patterns he'd been seeing his whole life? They connected SIX of the hardest math problems ever. The kid who "couldn't do math" had solved problems that stumped experts for 100 years.
