--- THE ONE-LINE ANSWER ---

The anomaly is a Z/3 deck-transformation grading.  Gamma_0(9) is NORMAL in
Gamma_0(3), the reflection j = diag(1,-1) inverts the deck group, so P.L is
block-off-diagonal on the level-9-new part and its spectrum is exactly
lambda <-> -lambda.  It is confined to the TRIVIAL-character operator.  The
chi-twisted operator used for Phase 2 is IRREDUCIBLE -- the deck group does
not act on it at all -- so the 29/29 result is NOT affected.  Re-measured.

--- STEP 2.  THE GRADING OPERATOR G ---

Found constructively, not by search.  Let

    gamma_0 = (1 0 ; 3 1) in Gamma_0(3),
    U       = LEFT multiplication by gamma_0 on Gamma_0(9)\PSL(2,Z),
    G       = (U - U^2)/(w - w^2),   w = exp(2 pi i / 3).

U is a signed-free permutation of the 12 cosets with the cycle structure of
the four fibres of P^1(Z/9) -> P^1(Z/3):

    U : (0,10,11)(1,7,4)(2,8,5)(3,9,6)      [cosets indexed as in rh_gamma09.PTS]

All of the following are EXACT (0.0 or float64 epsilon):

Pi_new = (2 - U - U^2)/3 is the projector on the 8-dim new block; G is zero on

of the two 3-cycles inside each fibre -- Hermitian, purely imaginary entries

--- STEP 3.  WHAT G IS, GROUP-THEORETICALLY ---

  phi(a b ; c d) = (c/3) * d^{-1}  mod 3

is a HOMOMORPHISM Gamma_0(3) -> Z/3 with kernel Gamma_0(9).  Measured: 0
failures over 400 x 400 products in Gamma_0(3); image {0,1,2}; phi(gamma_0)=1.
So Gamma_0(9) is normal in Gamma_0(3), X_0(9) -> X_0(3) is a Z/3 Galois cover,
and Gamma_0(3) lies inside the normalizer of Gamma_0(9) in PSL(2,R).

Also measured: phi(j g j^-1) = -phi(g) on 600 elements -- the reflection
INVERTS the deck group.  That single fact is the whole anomaly:

  Ind_{Gamma_0(9)}^{PSL(2,Z)} 1  =  Ind_{Gamma_0(3)} 1        (4-dim, U = 1)
                                (+) Ind_{Gamma_0(3)} psi      (4-dim, U = w)
                                (+) Ind_{Gamma_0(3)} psi-bar  (4-dim, U = w^2)

L preserves each piece (it is built from rho alone).  P swaps the last two
(because j inverts phi, hence conjugates psi to psi-bar).  Therefore P.L is
off-diagonal on their sum, and its spectrum there is +-paired.

This CONFIRMS the hypothesis on record, and pins it.  h = largest divisor of
24 with h^2 | N: h = 3 for N = 9, h = 1 for N = 3.  The extra normalizer
element is exactly Gamma_0(N/h) = Gamma_0(3) acting as deck transformations,
and Gamma_0(3) (h = 1) has no analogue -- Gamma_0(3) is not normal in
PSL(2,Z), so no U exists at level 3.  Confirmed numerically by the level-3
control in Step 1.

The three listed alternatives, resolved:
  * "the new block splits as V (+) V* with P.L off-diagonal"  -- TRUE, and it
    is the same statement as the normalizer one, not a competing explanation.
    V = Ind(psi), V* = Ind(psi-bar).
  * "the block mixes chi and chi-bar"  -- NOT the mechanism.  The anomalous
    block belongs to the TRIVIAL-character operator, where no chi is present.
    The mixed characters are psi, psi-bar of Gamma_0(3), not chi mod 9.
  * "P is mis-specified for p^2"  -- NO.  P satisfies P^2 = 1,
    P rho(S) = rho(S) P, P rho(T) = rho(T^-1) P exactly (V2, original run), and
    the anomaly is fully accounted for without altering P.  There is no
    correction to make: a Laplace eigenvalue living in Ind(psi) has NO
    well-defined reflection parity, because the reflection carries Ind(psi)
    onto a different subspace.  The +- split of the Selberg zeta simply does
    not refine the level-9-new part.  That is geometry, not a bug in P.

--- 4e.  WHAT THE ANOMALY DOES AFFECT: PHASE 1's MULTIPLICITY BOOKKEEPING ---

Because P.L = [[0,A],[B,0]] on the new block,

    det(1 - P.L) det(1 + P.L) |_new = det(1 - AB) det(1 - BA) = det(1 - AB)^2

-- the level-9-new part enters the Selberg zeta as a PERFECT SQUARE.  Measured
(trivial character, N = 28, so the residuals below are truncation-limited):

  |----------------|---------------------------------------|-------------------|
  | 4.38805356322  |                              3.95e-08 |          7.06e-08 |
  | 5.09874190873  |                              5.06e-09 |          1.40e-08 |
  | 6.12057553309  |                              2.62e-09 |          6.57e-09 |
  | 6.75741527775  |                              9.43e-10 |          5.01e-09 |

Consequences, stated exactly:
  * A new-block zero of Z_{Gamma_0(9)} = det(1-PL) det(1+PL) always has EVEN
    order, one in each factor.  It carries no parity label.
  * rh_gamma09_results.txt Phase 1c counts "1 (4-dim) + 1 (8-dim) = 2" by
    keeping only the parity-correct member of each +- pair.  That is a
    convention imposed on an unlabelled pair, not a derived multiplicity.
    The locations of the zeros -- what Phase 1 was built to gate -- are
    unaffected, exactly as the original note said.
  * Nothing here touches Phase 2, which uses a different, irreducible
    operator with no such structure.
