13. Reading guide for a referee — twelve steps, apex-first

If you have never read Principia Fractalis before and you want to assess whether it works, read in this order. The order is "apex-first" — start with the theorem that unifies everything, then go to the pieces.
  1. Start at revision2_changes.tex. The five-label rigor convention (PROVEN / CONDITIONAL REDUCTION / ASSERTED / EMPIRICAL — UNTESTED / EXTERNAL ANCHOR) governs every claim in the book. Get comfortable with the labels before anything else.
  2. The apex is chapters/ch34A_substrate_theorem.tex. It bundles 5 antecedents (A1–A5, all proven) and 25 consequences (C1–C25) as one typed meta-theorem. If you only read one chapter, read ch34A.
  3. The substrate rigidity theorem (T_infinity_rigidity in PF/SubstrateRigidity.lean) is the single most load-bearing result in the corpus. First Lean 4 formalization of Glimm 1960 UHF classification specialized to supernatural number 3∞.
  4. The K-theoretic obstruction (pi_not_ktheoretic_ratio) is the mathematically sharp statement of "program vs. ToE": any substrate-to-observable map must route through σ or posit the α-skeleton. Diagram 8 in this document captures the picture.
  5. The nine α-values + fifteen invariants (PF/CrossMillenniumSharedInvariants.lean) are the algebraic skeleton. Two "view" theorems — constraint-satisfaction and genuinely-parameterized — show the skeleton is forced once any one of five pinning α's is chosen.
  6. The H3 Coxeter bridge (PF/NSAlphaFromH3ExponentSum_r340.lean) grounds both π-anchors in the same icosahedral exponent datum {1, 5, 9}. This is the point that eliminates circularity in the NS realization predicate.
  7. The six Clay chapters each open with a scope paragraph naming (a) what IS kernel-verified, (b) what is conditional on which named antecedents, (c) what is substrate reading / empirical. These are the referee-defensive entry points.
  8. The 2026-11-15 typed substrate capstone family (PF/Referee/*.lean) is the Lean-verified kernel for the eight-axis contract: Poincaré + Fermat + six open Clays. Scope caveat: the typed contract is NOT the full Clay statement; six Clay statements remain open at the analytic/geometric layer.
  9. For cosmology/consciousness honesty: Sections 5 and 6 of this document, plus ch26–ch32 of the book. Three cosmology predictions explicitly FAIL; one gives 0.86σ soft agreement. Consciousness threshold ch2 = 19/20 is proven as a framework anchor; its empirical validation is UNTESTED.
  10. For verification detail: ch34 Verification Chapter has dated entries from 2026-07-23 through 2026-11-15 recording every kernel-level landing.
  11. For specific Lean theorem lookups: Appendix I is the per-chapter cross-reference table.
  12. For the honest gap inventory: codex/FRAMEWORK_CLAIMS_GAP_INVENTORY_2026-11-15.md is the single source of truth on remaining open items. §XII documents the 2026-11-15 typed-capstone landings and what remains open.
