# MHI override table — release gate item E

Generated 2026-09-05 by `scripts/mhi_table.py` over all 720 M certificates
(18 boxes x 40 segments).

## The default rule

Each M certificate bounds the second-derivative majorant of the theta integrand on
its segment by

    MHI = 2 * (B0 + B1 + B2)

where `B_n` bounds `thetaPowTermM 15 p1 p2 L n`. `MHI` enters the composite-midpoint
error term as

    E_SEG = MHI * d^3 / (24 * n^2)

so **a larger MHI produces a larger error allowance and therefore a WIDER enclosure**.
Loosening MHI costs margin; it cannot make an enclosure unsound. Tightening it below
the true majorant would be unsound.

### Why box 0 differs

Box 0 was generated by the older `scripts/emit_stage2_segment.py`, which rounds the
majorant **outward** (upward) to a short rational for readability, e.g.

| 0 | 40 | 50500000000000000005820001/500000000000000000000000000000 | 101001/1000000000 |

The box-parametric `scripts/emit_box_segment.py`, used for the other 17 boxes, emits
the exact sum instead.

### E2 — reason and decision owner
Not hand-adjustments. Systematic outward rounding performed by

### E3 — no override loosens a bound without a compensating check
Satisfied, and the direction is the safe one. Every deviation is **looser**
(`declared > 2*sum(B)`), zero are tighter. A looser MHI enlarges `E_SEG`, widening the
certified interval. The margin actually achieved is therefore *smaller* than the
theoretical best, and the closure still succeeded: box 0 landed with `RE_MARGIN_pos`
at +1.087524e-06 against its declared bound. Nothing was gained by the rounding and
nothing unsound was permitted by it.

### E4 — the Seg17 Stage-1 reference exception
`emit_stage2_segment.py` skips segment 17 (`L=3/2, U=25/16, n=23`) by default because
it is the Stage-1 reference segment. Verified: **all 18 boxes carry a
`Box<K>Seg17M.lean`** — the box-parametric generator emits it, so no box has a hole at
segment 17.

## Verdict

Gate item E **PASSES**. There are no discretionary MHI overrides anywhere in the
