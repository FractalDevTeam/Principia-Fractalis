Five conditions. Not opinions — each is a command whose output I can paste.

1. **`gate_sweep.py` reports PRISTINE for 1452 of 1452.**
2. **The gate itself has stopped changing.** Pablo's own calibration: *"the
   signal to wait for is detection power stable AND find-rate on new work near
   zero."* A rising find-rate under rising detection power is healthy, not bad.
3. **New modules gate PRISTINE first-pass**, with no shadow theorems needing to
   be cut before shipping.
4. **Every chapter finding is either repaired or marked an explicit authorial
   decision** — nothing merely flagged. (`feedback-repair-not-disclosure`: a
