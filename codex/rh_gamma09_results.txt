=== WHY LEVEL 9 ===

M2 (2026-08-03) closed the Gamma_0(3) route with a NEGATIVE result: chi_3 mod 3
is the quadratic character and is ODD, so it is inadmissible as a weight-0
nebentypus and L(s, chi_3) cannot appear in Gamma_0(3) scattering.

That obstruction is level-3 only.  The order-3 characters mod 9 are EVEN:
-1 = 8 = 2^3 in (Z/9)* and chi(2)^3 = 1, so chi(-1) = +1.  They are primitive
mod 9 (an order-3 character cannot be induced from mod 3, whose unit group has
order 2).  Weight-0 admissible.  Verified three independent ways below.

=== THE CONSTRUCTION ===

  Cosets Gamma_0(9)\PSL(2,Z) <-> P^1(Z/9), 12 of them.  Index 12 (Gamma_0(3)
  was 4).  Induced representation of the nebentypus:

      rho(g)_{ij} = chi(R_i g R_j^{-1})  if R_i g R_j^{-1} lies in Gamma_0(9),
                    0                    otherwise,          chi(gamma) := chi(d)

  -- monomial, 12-dimensional.  It descends to PSL(2,Z) exactly because chi is
  even: rho(S)^2 = rho(-I) = chi(-1) I.

      (L_s f)(x) = sum_{k>=1} (x+k)^{-2s} rho(S T^k) f(1/(x+k))

  rho(T) has order 9 in all three characters (measured), so the k-sum splits by
  k mod 9 into nine scalar blocks A_r tensored with rho(S T^r), with exact
  Hurwitz-zeta matrix elements in M1's disc-normalized basis, R = 3/2.

  M2's lesson carried: the UNTWISTED det(1-L)det(1+L) is NOT the Selberg zeta
  for Gamma_0(N).  Fraczek-Mayer Thm 2.1.1's operator P (from j = diag(1,-1),
  which normalizes Gamma_0(9) and fixes the nebentypus because it leaves d
  alone) is inserted:  Z(s, chi) = det(1 - P L_s) det(1 + P L_s).

M2's own Gamma_0(3) operator, evaluated at the same parameters, gives O(1) in

8-dim level-9-new block.  It is not explained here.  The leading hypothesis --

split the 8-dim block further.

ESTABLISHED

1. The order-3 characters mod 9 are even and weight-0 admissible.  Checked
   three ways: chi(8) = +1 directly; L(0,chi) = 0 (a trivial zero at s = 0 is
   the signature of an EVEN character); and rho(S)^2 = rho(-I) = chi(-1) I = I
   inside the 12-dim induced representation, which is what makes the whole
   construction descend to PSL(2,Z) at all.

2. The Gamma_0(9) transfer operator was built and self-checked: 12 cosets,
   S and T permutations, rho a homomorphism, rho(ST)^3 = 1, rho restricted to
   Gamma_0(9) equal to chi, trivial chi giving back the permutation rep, and
   the Fraczek-Mayer twist P satisfying P^2 = 1, P rho(S) = rho(S) P,
   P rho(T) = rho(T^-1) P.  All to 1e-40.

3. THE GATE PASSES.  The trivial-character Gamma_0(9) determinant contains all
   ten M2-certified Gamma_0(3)/Gamma_0(1) spectral parameters as zeros of the
   parity-correct factor, positioned to ~4e-12 in t, with exactly the predicted
   oldform multiplicities (2 for a level-3 newform, 3 for the level-1 form).
   The 4-dim Gamma_0(3) sub-block reproduces M2 eigenvalue by eigenvalue.

4. The numerics are gated, not asserted: float64 determinant error is 1e-6 of
   the truncation error; truncation drift N=56 vs N=64 is 1.4e-9
   scale-normalized; dps=40 is converged against dps=55 (dps=30 is not).

5. THE ANSWER TO THE QUESTION ASKED.  All 29 verified zeros of L(s, chi_9)
   appear in det(1 - P L_s^{chi}) at s = rho/2 on Re s = 1/4.  Ratios against
   local controls: 3.0e-14 to 1.6e-10.  Zero of them appear in the odd factor
   (ratios 0.29 - 1.34), and zero of them appear anywhere in the
   trivial-character Gamma_0(9) operator at the same 29 points (ratios
   0.42 - 3.19).  The chi-twist is what brings the L-function in.  The zeros
   sit on rho/2 to 3e-14 and 6e-15 at the two points profiled, and they are
   SIMPLE (|det| = c |delta t| over six decades).

   M2's negative result at level 3 was not a dead end in the framework's
   ternary structure; it was specific to the parity of chi_3 mod 3.  Moving to
   the first level where an order-3 character is EVEN produces the L-function
   the level-3 route could not.

NOT ESTABLISHED

a. Nothing about the Riemann Hypothesis.  Nothing about Hilbert-Polya.  The
   zeros studied in Phase 2 live on the EISENSTEIN / scattering side, which is
   exactly the side on which Hilbert-Polya fails.  A determinant that sees
   L-function zeros on Re s = 1/4 is an instrument fact about the scattering
   determinant, not a spectral realisation of anything.

b. The opposite-parity extra zero in the 8-dim level-9-new block (Phase 1c) is
   NOT explained.  It is localized and reproducible, and absent at level 3.
   The hypothesis that j = diag(1,-1) is an incomplete symmetry operator for
   Gamma_0(p^2) was NOT tested; W_9 and the extra p^2 normalizer element were
   not built.

c. No external reference data for level 9 exists in LMFDB (queried, no
   matches), so the Phase 1 gate rests entirely on the internal oldform
   structure.  That is a strong gate but it is not an independent measurement
   of a level-9 quantity.

d. Level-9 NEWform Maass parameters (trivial or nebentypus) were not
   identified.  The scan's unmatched minima were not followed up.

e. The scan-with-refinement version of Phase 1 has a fragile minimum-finder
   (it bailed on 6 of 10 parameters), and the Phase 2 parabolic refiner
   overshoots on simple zeros (all 4 attempts landed 2-3e-5 off and reported a
   LARGER |det| than the value at gamma/2).  Both were superseded by direct
   evaluation and profiling, not fixed.

f. The identification "det(1 - P L) is the EVEN factor" is inherited from the
   level-1 and level-3 conventions and is consistent with everything measured,
   but at level 9 the parity LABEL is compromised by the anomaly in (b).  What
   is measured without any parity assumption is: the L-zeros land in the
   det(1 - P L) factor and not the det(1 + P L) factor.

g. The scattering matrix for (Gamma_0(9), chi) was NOT derived here.  The
   expectation that L(2s, chi) sits in its denominator -- and hence that the
   determinant should vanish rather than blow up at s = rho/2 -- was taken from
   the level-1 analogue (Lewis-Zagier) and then CONFIRMED empirically, not
   proved.  The 29/29 agreement is evidence for the identification, not a
   derivation of it.

  - Build W_9 and the Gamma_0(p^2) normalizer element; test whether they split
    the 8-dim block and remove the opposite-parity partner.
  - Derive, rather than assume, which factor the L(2s,chi) zeros must sit in,
    from the explicit scattering matrix for (Gamma_0(9), chi primitive).
  - Identify the level-9 newform spectrum from the unmatched scan minima; it
    would be new data, since LMFDB has none.
