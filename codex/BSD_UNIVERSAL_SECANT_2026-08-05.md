**r195 — the content bridge (the structural discovery).**
On the diagonal the secant forms ARE r174's duplication pair:
`Sfz(a,b,a,b) = Psi(a,b)`, `Prz(a,b,a,b) = Phi(a,b)`, `DDz(a,b,a,b) = 0`.
Eliminating the second point against `DDz` produces exactly the SQUARES of
the duplication pair at the first point, with small integer Bézout
cofactors (from `Res_{x₂}(DD,Sf) = ψ(x₁)²`, `Res_{x₂}(DD,Pr) = φ(x₁)²`):
`sfCert₁·DDz + sfCert₂·Sfz = b₂³·Psi₁²` and the `Prz`/`Phi` analogue.
The secant content problem reduces to the r174 keystone `Res(Φ,Ψ) = Δ²`.

**r196 — the universal secant content bound.**

**r198 — the universal secant upper window, point level.**
On ANY Weierstrass curve over ℚ with integer b-invariants, for affine
points with distinct x-coordinates, over mathlib's group law:

  `H(x(P+Q))·H(x(P−Q)) ≤ 2·CUsec·H(x₁)²·H(x₂)²`,
  `CUsec = max(4, 4+|b₂|+2|b₄|+|b₆|, 1+|b₄|+2|b₆|+|b₈|)`.

Chain: r181 `addX` identities → r193 specs (integer quadratic relations
`DDz·(x₃+x₄) = Sfz`, `DDz·x₃x₄ = Prz`: `sum_relation`, `prod_relation`) →
r148e `naiveHeight_mul_le_of_quadratic` → r193 size bounds. Gated on 15
real 389a1 point pairs (exact rational group law).

## Frictions (this arc's ledger additions)

- sympy `gcdex` over `QQ.frac_field` gives Bézout cofactors; clearing
  denominators = resultant-power weights; Sylvester gives BOTH monomial
  weights (`a₂³` and `b₂³`) — the corner cancellation needs both.
- `positivity` fails on `|B₂^2|`-sums (use `repeat' apply add_nonneg`);
  `positivity` takes NO arguments.
- r175's `disc_sq_mul_le` has IMPLICIT b-invariants (section variables).
- abs-of-square: `abs_of_nonneg (sq_nonneg _)`, not `sq_abs`.
- r193's `Sfz_spec`/`Prz_spec` need explicit `(a₁ := x₁.num) (a₂ := x₂.num)`.
- `naiveHeight` cast: `rw [naiveHeight, Nat.cast_max, Int.abs_eq_natAbs]`.
- Generated-proof pattern that works: per-term `abs_le.mp (term_bound …)`
  facts + `linarith` (it ring-normalizes atoms consistently).
