CAVEATS, stated rather than buried:
 * the (w0,wa) errors are treated as independent; DESI reports them
   strongly anticorrelated, so the true tensions differ from these.
   The SIGN result (Result 1) is immune to that.
 * Lambda_eff back-reaction on H(a) is neglected: H is pure LCDM.
 * "one factor per level" is the simplest tower ansatz, not forced.
