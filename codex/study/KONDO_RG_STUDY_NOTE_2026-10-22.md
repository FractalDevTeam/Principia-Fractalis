## The paper (exact citation)
Tianyu Zhu (Yale), Linqing Peng (Caltech), Huanchen Zhai, Runze Chi, Zhi-Hao Cui; senior author Garnet Chan (Caltech).
"Toward an exact quantum many-body treatment of Kondo correlation in magnetic impurities," *Science*, July 30 issue (2026). DOI 10.1126/science.adq7402.
Sources read by Rio: Caltech news release (institutional, quotes the authors) + ScienceAlert report. The paper's own abstract was NOT read (fetch failed) — bibliographic facts and claims below are as the Caltech release states them.

## What was done
- Problem: the Kondo effect — a magnetic impurity (e.g., iron, manganese) in a metal; below the Kondo temperature, conduction electrons collectively screen the impurity's magnetism (the "Kondo cloud"), and resistance bottoms out and rises instead of falling.
- Prior state: qualitative understanding since the 1970s (Kenneth Wilson, Caltech PhD '61, central to it); quantitative treatment only via simplified models — reduce the material to a few orbitals, apply an approximate model.
- This work: quantum-chemistry machinery applied to the material directly — the impurity described fully, as a molecule, with the material's actual atomic and electronic structure, no reduced model.
- Result: seven transition-metal atoms embedded in copper; for most, predictions beat model-based calculations by up to two orders of magnitude in accuracy, against experiment.

## Why this is a study input (the one real thread)
The Kondo problem is the historical birthplace of Wilson's numerical renormalization group (1975) — the work that turned the renormalization group into a computational method and won the Nobel Prize. RG is the established mathematics of scale: iterated coarse-graining, flow, fixed points, universality — and scale invariance at fixed points is the same intellectual territory as self-similarity and fractal structure in critical phenomena. This is the PF fractal study's "established mathematics first" track, not an analogy: the study assignment below is to read the RG treatment of Kondo on its own merits.

Study questions (for the study track, in order):
1. Wilson's NRG for Kondo: what is iterated, what flows, what is the fixed point, and what exactly is universal at it?
2. The Kondo temperature is an emergent scale — it appears in no bare parameter and arises non-perturbatively (T_K scales exponentially in the inverse coupling). Where else does the corpus rely on an emergent scale, and is the mechanism the same kind?
3. The screening cloud: a coherent structure whose size is set by the emergent scale (ξ ~ ħv_F / k_B T_K), potentially microns from an atomic cause. Record the size claim only from a primary source before any use.
