classical fact being formalized, the alpha-cascade is postulates, the physics is not
connected to the substrate. There is no race there to lose. The asset that CAN be
taken is the operator-algebra work (3^inf UHF/Glimm classification, Noether-Skolem

substrate work becomes a footnote. Defense = dated, named, external record (merged
PR, arXiv preprint), not faster Millennium work. And if a Millennium result is ever
real, only the version with no `Prop := True` near it will be believed — so the
pristine/gate work IS the road, not a detour.
