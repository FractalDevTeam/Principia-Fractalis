\begin{proof}[Derivation chain]
The proof is a linear chain through the invariants:
\begin{enumerate}
\item From (I11) and (I3): $\alpha_{YM} \pi = 2\pi$, so
$\alpha_{YM} = 2$.
\item From (I7) and step 1: $\alpha_{\mathrm{Poincar\acute{e}}} =

$\alpha_P = \sqrt{\alpha_{YM}} = \sqrt{2}$.

\item From (I10) and step 5: $\alpha_{NP} = \alpha_{\mathrm{Hodge}} + 1/4
= \varphi + 1/4$.
\item From (I3) and $\alpha_{QG} > 0$: $\alpha_{QG} = \sqrt{2\pi}$.
\item From (I13), (I3): $(8/3) \alpha_{BSD} = 2\pi$, so
$\alpha_{BSD} = 3\pi/4$.
\item From (I5) and step 8: $\alpha_{NS} = 2 \alpha_{BSD} = 3\pi/2$.
