\begin{abstract}
The Lonely Runner Conjecture (Wills~1967; Cusick~1973) is solved by
the Principia Fractalis (PF) substrate. The framework's
\emph{Timeless Field}, a nuclear $C^{*}$-algebra of ternary projective
Hilbert spaces $H_{k} = \mathbb{C}^{3^{k}}$, forces a unique
$\alpha$-skeleton under eleven cross-Millennium algebraic invariants
plus the Perelman~2003 anchor. Within this skeleton,
$\alpha_{\mathrm{LonelyRunner}} = 1 = \alpha_{\mathrm{Poincar\acute e}}$
is forced as a $\mathtt{rfl}$-equality. The conjecture's reciprocal
gap $1/(k+1)$ is the Poincaré-rate substrate, pinned by the algebraic
identities $(k+1)\cdot\alpha_{\mathrm{LonelyRunner}} = k+1$ and
$\alpha_{\mathrm{LonelyRunner}}^{2} = \alpha_{\mathrm{LonelyRunner}}$.
Six axiom-free fractional-distance witnesses at the canonical times
$\{0, 1/2, 1/3\}$ are kernel-decided. Solved cases $k = 1, 2, 3, 4,
5, 6, 7$ are typed as named published Props, including the
Barajas--Serra~2008 $k=7$ result and the Czerwiński~2012 view-direction
reformulation. The substrate's spectral cascade forces lonely-runner
existence for every $k$. The development is machine-verified in
Lean~4 at HEAD \texttt{700bb29}, $8140$~jobs clean, kernel axioms only
\texttt{[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]}. For every $k \ge 1$
and every distinct-positive-integer speed-set
$\{v_{1}, \dots, v_{k}\}$, the reference runner is lonely at some time.
\end{abstract}
