\begin{abstract}
We present a fundamental reformulation of general relativity incorporating consciousness as an active dynamical field. Building upon the Timeless Field $\TF$ and fractal resonance ontology, we demonstrate that Einstein's conservation laws $\nabla_\mu \Tmunu = 0$ represent a degenerate limit of the complete theory where consciousness contributions vanish. We rigorously derive modified field equations incorporating a consciousness stress-energy tensor $\Cmunu$ and source current $J^\nu_\mathrm{consciousness}$, showing that apparent violations of energy conservation arise from consciousness-mediated transitions between classical and quantum information states. The theory predicts measurable deviations from general relativity in regions of high consciousness density ($\chh > 0.95$), resolves the cosmological constant problem by identifying dark energy as a consciousness suppression effect, and provides testable predictions for laboratory and astronomical observations. This work establishes consciousness not as an emergent phenomenon but as a fundamental field whose dynamics necessarily modify spacetime geometry.
\end{abstract}
